The Thing in the Corridor

Four teams. Twenty doors. Something behind you that never stops walking.

The beat

  1. The door's clue appears. Read it aloud once, in a low voice.
  2. Everybody writes. Thirty seconds, silent. Every student, every team.
  3. Draw a number. That seat number answers for all four teams at once — each team's Number 6, say, holds up their board.
  4. Mark each team right or wrong. Right moves you two doors.
  5. The Thing moves one door. Always.

The rule that does the work

Any team you hear talking during the writing phase: press Noise. They lose a door and The Thing moves an extra one — towards everyone. The class polices itself after about two beats.

Caught is not out

A caught team becomes The Chorus. Each beat they may whisper one plausible but wrong answer, in English, to any team they choose. Nobody sits idle.
